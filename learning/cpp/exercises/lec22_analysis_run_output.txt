=== 6. the one line to remember ===
  Big-O, Omega and Theta are not descriptions of code, they are a constant and a threshold
  that can be checked by evaluating both sides: this run certified f(n) <= 1*g(n) from n = 7
  and f(n) >= (3/4)*g(n) from n = 1 for f(n) = 3n^2 + 5n + 7 against g(n) = 4n^2, and the
  certificate breaks the moment the constant crosses 3/4, the ratio of leading coefficients.
  Counting is how the complexity is found and how it is checked: the nested loop above ran
  1048576 times at n = 1024 and the two triangular loops 523776 each, both measured and both
  matched against their closed forms.
  Best and worst case are two keys into one function, 1 comparison against 1024, and the
  average over every possible key is 9224/17 = 542.588235, a weighted mix of the two, not a
  third speed. The growth table is why the exponent matters more than the constant: over one
  decade of n, n grows by 10, n^2 by 100 and 2^n by 2^90.
