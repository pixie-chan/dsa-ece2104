=== 9. the one line to remember ===
  a recursive call does not create a new problem, it suspends the current frame on the
  call stack, and the frame is charged per suspended call: 48 bytes for
  linearSumRec, 48 for factorial, 96 for hanoi, 32 for the runaway above. every one of those is the number measured in sections 2 and 8, never a typed constant.
  Depth times that number is your stack use, and it is released in reverse order when the
  base case is reached and the frames unwind.
